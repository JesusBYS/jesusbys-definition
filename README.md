# JesusBYS

JesusBYS is an independent Christian media and community project focused on inner transformation, personal growth, and lasting peace through the teachings of Jesus Christ.

The project produces digital content including music, written articles, videos, and community programs aimed at everyday spiritual and ethical life.

JesusBYS emphasizes practical faith, inner healing, non violence, and personal responsibility rather than political power or institutional control.

It is founded and led by its original creator and developed with the long term support of a dedicated team.

JesusBYS positions itself as a complementary initiative to traditional churches by offering accessible content adapted to modern digital culture.
